# Serial Dilution

- Work from highest concentration to lowest concentration.
- Add dilution buffer first when that reduces contamination risk, then transfer stock or previous dilution.
- Mix after each dilution step with a volume that is within pipette capacity and below well volume.
- Use a fresh tip between dilution points unless the protocol explicitly permits same-sample carryover.
- Record the dilution series direction and concentration labels in setup notes.
- Mirror dilution buffer and transferred sample totals in `manifest.reagents`.
