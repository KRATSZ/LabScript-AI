# PCR Setup

- Keep pre-PCR setup separate from post-PCR or amplified material.
- Use a PCR plate or strip labware plus cold storage for master mix, primers, enzymes, or template when required.
- Add master mix, primer mix, and template in a consistent order with fresh tips for templates and controls.
- Include positive controls, negative controls, and no-template controls when the task asks for them.
- Keep master mix, primer, template, and control totals aligned between protocol loops and `manifest.reagents`.
- Do not encode unsafe cost-saving tip reuse for templates or contaminated materials.
